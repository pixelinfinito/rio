[v0.1 report](v0.1.md), [aggregate](v0.1.json), and three independent automated agent
records remain unchanged. The new normalized records reuse their adjudication
evidence and the matching preserved Rio reports; no new human verdicts or public

**All 10 PRs are environment failures. Zero are eligible.** Baseline type/lint
classification was unavailable in every case: immer's root installation mismatch
and trusted nested project, pydantic-settings' trusted installation after coverage
`.pth` verification failed, and structlog's absent lockfile/trusted tools. A completed
frozen install did not establish compatibility for Rio. None of these runs can
establish clean-change usefulness or scored product precision. The old 1/10 useful
judgment becomes 0 eligible observations, not a new 0/10 usefulness result.

The 4430 old `noise` verdicts map to **pre-existing-shown**, distinct from four
false-positive findings. The classification inherits the old reviewers' evidence:
immer has a base diagnostic reproduction; structlog has unchanged-line evidence;
pydantic-settings identifies tests outside the declared mypy target. The latter
is not a new per-diagnostic base reproduction, and no claim of successful Rio
baseline classification is made. All remain excluded observations. No synthetic
finding is invented for a skipped check; skips live in the environment ledger.

There are **four broad operational families**, not 4430 independent defects:

- immer's incompatible installation and test-project TypeScript configuration;
- pydantic-settings' overly broad `mypy .` invocation outside its intended package target;
- structlog's trusted, unpinned tools exposing existing documentation/style diagnostics;
- secret heuristics producing incorrect credential alerts.

These families are explanatory. The stricter requested
`rule_id + message_template + file_set` dedupe yields **191 per-PR cause keys**
(188 pre-existing-shown, 3 false-positive), or **66 unique repository/cause keys**
(64 pre-existing-shown, 2 false-positive). Historical template overrides replace quoted payloads and numeric
positions; file sets union equal rule/template diagnostics within each shared setup
cause. This grouping is justified by the old configuration-level adjudications,
not enabled as a default for new runs. Exact messages, locations, and finding IDs
remain in each compressed raw record. Repeated cause keys across PRs count once
per PR in observation totals; the unique column dedupes across a repository.
The four immer PRs repeat an identical environment problem: they add no independent
eligible evidence. Three repository setups, zero eligible setups, are a better
account of the original sample's independence.

**Both reported misses are out of scope for the deterministic layer**: immer's
filter/deleted-element semantics and structlog's non-weak-referenceable writer
compatibility. Their independent reproductions establish semantic regressions;
they do not show an enabled type/lint rule, coverage gap, secret, or vulnerable
dependency oracle could catch them. They appear in the agent-layer backlog and
do not reduce deterministic recall. There were no registered positive controls;
reviewing correct maintainer fixes did not give precision a true-positive
opportunity or provide a recall denominator. The two incidental semantic
regressions do not repair that sample design. Precision and recall are undefined.

**Real product issues remain.** Two dummy localhost connection-string fixture
alerts in pydantic-settings (#1109 and #1097) are incorrect secret claims and have
no remediation confirmation in this evidence. They recur as one unique repo/cause
key. The two ContextVar-identifier alerts in structlog #805 were also genuine
false positives, but a later different-binary
[confirmation](evidence/structlog/pr-805-confirmation.json) shows them removed;
they must not be reported as current defects. They share one normalized key in
that PR. Overly broad mypy target selection is a real configuration/adapter
usability defect even when diagnostics are honest. Installation verification
compatibility and support for baseline classification on these real projects
remain blockers to evaluation and warrant product investigation; the evidence
does not prove that every conservative mismatch is a Rio bug. Unpinned tool drift
and missing project lockfiles also require evaluation setup repair. Earlier graph
and process-performance fixes remain historical observations, not current failures.

The original no-go was not evidence of near-zero product precision or two
in-scope missed bugs. The corrected conclusion is **insufficient evidence;
release remains blocked** pending repaired environments, at least five repositories,
both languages, independent clean controls, and the preregistered positive controls.
