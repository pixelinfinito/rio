# rio/council: A/B on the plausible bucket (2026-11-09)

**Status: run complete; keep-or-remove verdict pending owner adjudication.** The
verdict turns on one finding that has no label yet (the one the council promoted), so
this record gives the gate's result under each label and leaves the decision to the
owner. It is the evidence for task 7.3 of
[`rio-v0-3-council`](../../openspec/changes/rio-v0-3-council/tasks.md). The live

## Setup

- **Cases.** Every labeled reference case whose run of record produced a `plausible`

- **Arms.** Two profiles identical except the council step:
  [`ab-baseline`](v0.3-council/profiles/ab-baseline.yaml) (verifier-only, step
  disabled) and [`ab-council`](v0.3-council/profiles/ab-council.yaml) (one trigger,
  `evidence == plausible`, `max_findings: 5`, `timeout: 240s`). Both extend
  `rio/security`; agents on Sonnet 5.5 and voters on GPT 6.1 Sol through the account's
  AI Gateway custom providers, 3 voters, quorum 2, fast tier (2.4M review tokens,
  12 min).
- **Council.** The five built-in members filled from the endpoint's allowlist, all via
  OpenRouter: skeptic and tester Sonnet 5.5, exploit-author GPT 6.1 Sol, code-owner
  Kimi K3.1 Pro, maintainer Gemini 4 Argon.
- **Endpoint.** Deployed from `e3d8092` with runner image `rio-runner:0.3.1-council`.
  That build predates `optimize-llm-prompt-caching`: it is an ancestor of that
  change's pinned baseline `2ac7b22`, so neither the tenant partition line nor any
  prompt-cache hint moved this A/B. A rerun for the verdict must also use a build
  at or before `2ac7b22`.
- **Run.** [`v0.3-council/drive.py`](v0.3-council/drive.py), three cases at a time,
  verifier-only arm first and council arm second per case, same day. Raw reports,
  audits and stored member answers are kept privately under
  `~/.local/share/rio/v0.3-council/ab/2026-11-09/` on the maintainer machine and
  passed `eval/credscan.py`.
- **Scoring.** `eval/council.py summarize` and `eval/gate.py council`. Outcomes,
  summaries and gate results are in [`v0.3-council/`](v0.3-council/).

## Run notes

- **CT-POS-04 and HS-TS-01, council arm:** the CLI lost its connection while polling
  (`Connection reset by peer`). Both reviews completed on the endpoint; their reports
  were fetched from `GET /v1/reviews/<id>/report`, and the audits and member answers
  from the usual sources.
- **CT-POS-05:** both arms ended `partial` before the agents step with "Worker
  exceeded CPU time limit" on foundry's impact graph, at zero model cost, once in the
  parallel batch and again alone. The 2026-11-07 run of record completed this case,
  so this may be a regression on the branch; it is not caused by the council (the
  verifier-only arm fails the same way). The case contributes no finding.
- **CL-RS-02:** the verifier-only arm produced no plausible finding, and the council
  arm's security agent returned a malformed result; the case contributes no finding.
- **CT-POS-04, council arm:** 9 of the 10 member calls abstained with cause `budget`,
  because agents and voters on this 107 MiB repository had already spent the shared
  2.4M-token review budget (the review cost USD 4.86). The council does not get its
  own budget; on large changes it is starved first.

## Cost

  endpoint's usage ledger. The CT-POS-05 reruns cost nothing.
- Council calls alone: USD 2.14 over 10 selected findings (USD 0.06 of it on the two

  versions, 7 on head only) and 5 held back by `max_experiments`.
- Per case, the council arm cost USD −0.17 to +0.53 against verifier-only; beyond the
  council calls, the difference is verifier and agent variance.

## Reading

- The disagreement condition passes by a wide margin (50 % against the 5 % floor), so
  the council is not a rubber stamp on this set.
- The accuracy condition rests on a single promotion. The set cannot test refutation
  (no `not_real` finding), and the one promotion is a manifest fact whose harm is a
  judgment. A keep on this evidence would be a keep by one case.
- Most experiments that ran were on head only (7 of 11). Only a run on both
  versions can promote a finding, and the one promotion came from such a run. Asking
  members for base-and-head experiments is the obvious prompt change before the next
  cycle.
- The council shares the review budget, so it is starved on large changes
  (CT-POS-04).
