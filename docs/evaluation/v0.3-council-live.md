## Defect found and fixed

Three round-2 answers on the `package.json` finding abstained as `invalid`: Gemini changed its `basis` (static_path → reproduction, citing `exp-1`) and put `basis` in `changed_from`/`changed_to`, which the schema did not allow although the round-2 rule already treats a basis change as a position change; two Sonnet answers sent bare strings (`"unknown"` → `"yes"`). The schema now accepts `basis` in a position, and every member prompt shows the shape of `changed_from`/`changed_to` with an example.
