## Failures

These are product failures. They are reported here and were not adjudicated around,
and the two cases count neither as detections nor as misses.

1. **A malformed tool batch fails the whole review (CT-POS-01, CT-POS-04).** The
   cause is `converse` in `worker/src/gateway.ts`.
   - On CT-POS-01, the security agent requested 12 tools in one turn, and
     `converse` accepts 1 to 8.
   - On CT-POS-04, the security agent drifted into native tool-call syntax, then
     returned `{"tools":[]}`.
   - In both cases `converse` throws `malformed tool output` and the Workflow ends
     the review `partial`, discarding the other agent's finished work. On CT-POS-04
     the contracts agent had already produced a risk on the `guild_token` schema
     change.
   - Spend on these two cases: USD 2.92. CT-POS-04's first attempt failed earlier,
     in the runner (`Network connection lost`), before any model call; its retry hit
     the failure above.
   - Fix direction (not done in this run): trim or repair an oversized or empty tool
     batch the way unparseable replies are repaired, and let one agent's failure end
     only that agent.
2. **Verifier votes ran out of budget (CT-POS-03: 3 of 6 votes; CT-POS-05: 9 of
   12).** The review-wide 1.2M-token budget of `rio/openrouter` is sized for one
   agent. With the contracts and security agents both running, the ledger was
   exhausted before every voter finished. The reports are `incomplete` with the
   missing votes counted; their findings stay at the level the completed votes
   support. A `rio/security` deployment needs a larger review budget than
   `rio/openrouter` provides.
3. **Found and fixed during the pilot: the contracts agent was never selected for a
   Python CLI change (CT-POS-02).** Triage's `public_contracts` signal compared only
   TypeScript `export` and Rust `pub` declaration lines.
   - Commit `5febaeb` bases the signal on the inventory's contract surface, and the
     Worker was redeployed.
   - CT-POS-02 was rerun; its pre-fix pilot result is kept privately and not scored.
   - No other case ran before the fix.
